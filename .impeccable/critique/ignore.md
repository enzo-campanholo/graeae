# Critique: decided, do not raise

The owner ruled on these on 2026-11-18, in a grilling before the second critique pass (ticket 32). Each one stays as built. Drop any finding that asks to change one of them. Each line gives the call, then the reason it stays.

## Ticket 28's taste calls

- **The dim "Watch" label before Webcams / Everything / Nothing.** The row reads as one sentence ("Watch nothing"), names the group for screen readers, and adds no click.
- **A short last row of tiles centred under the rows above.** A short row centred under packed rows reads as one group of pictures; the stage is not a table, so column alignment gives way.
- **"Screen live" resting as a secondary (raised, muted, not dimmed), like "Webcam live".** A live share is a state, not an action. Both live states rest alike, and "one filled button per surface" means at most one, so a room with none is fine.
- **The owner page's content column (`--owner`, 1000px since ticket 34) centred in its panel.** The owner page reads as a centred settings column, like the plain pages and Linear's preferences. (Whether the member panel's weight balances the list at 1920 is still open to critique.)

## The stage's tiles

- **Every tile is a 16:9 frame, with the picture letterboxed inside (`object-contain`), not sized to the picture's own shape.** Even frames keep the grid calm and still, and a picture limited by height loses nothing to the letterbox. A portrait window or a 4:3 webcam with bars is intended.

## Earlier tickets' choices

- **06: a reconnecting member gets a dim "· reconnecting" and a breathing avatar, with no colour.** Reconnecting is someone else's state, not a warning to you. Grey words and a slow breath say it without alarm.
- **08: the Rooms section shows addresses without the scheme.** The shown address drops the scheme to stay one quiet line; the copy button copies it whole.
- **08: a new room gives access to the owner alone.** A new room is private until the owner grants it, and the line under "Create room" says so.
- **09: a member unticked from their only room stays a member with no room.** Removal from graeae is only ever chosen, never a side effect of a tick.
- **09: the removal modal for an absent member leaves out "within about 2 seconds".** Nothing is cut off, so no time is promised.
- **09: the confirm's Remove rests in the stop tint, unlike Stop and Forget.** The tint marks the one action that can't be undone, in a dialog that exists for it. Cancel holds the focus.
- **14: "Webcams" and "Everything" replace the stage rather than add to it.** A shortcut lands on a known stage, not a sum.
- **14: clicking a name that shares nothing follows that member and empties the stage.** Following is about a person. The empty stage says what will show.
- **14: clicking the followed name again does not unfollow.** The name only ever goes somewhere. "Nothing", another name or an icon ends following.
- **14: the column table (1; 2 up to four; 3 up to six; 4 beyond) stays as the spec fixes it.** The spec fixes the table, and the 1080p promise is measured against it.
- **16: the volume is kept per member on the device, and mute is forgotten with the watch.** A volume is a standing taste. A mute belongs to the moment, and a kept one would silence a share without a word.
- **16: "Hear only" is an action, not a mode.** Nothing is turned down by itself, and the same button undoes it.
- **16: a muted share's caption reads "screen · muted".** The sound's state reads from the caption at rest.
- **16: a webcam's caption stays "webcam", with no "· video only".** Webcams never carry sound, so nothing needs saying.
- **18: a dismissed note comes back only after it goes away by itself and later holds again.** It returns only as a new occurrence, so it never nags.
- **18: notes are grey, not amber, and there are none in the rail.** Notes block nothing, and amber means a warning. They live under the previews, which the rail does not have.
- **24: viewers click again once watching is allowed.** A raised budget never restarts watching by itself, so an idle tab spends nothing.
- **24: the owner's 80% usage strip sits in the room.** It is the one owner-only thing in the room, because a surprise bill is what the product promises never to cause.
