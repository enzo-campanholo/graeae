# 19: Hand-over between devices

**What to build:** A member with a screen share live on one device presses "Share screen" on another and is offered to stop it there and share from here. Viewers in the same room are carried over to the new share after a short gap. The same holds for the webcam.

Spec: [Shares](../spec.md#shares). User stories 57 to 59.

UI: build with the impeccable skill, following `DESIGN.md`.

**Blocked by:** 15

**Status:** resolved

- [x] One screen share and one webcam share per member across the whole instance, not per room and not per device
- [x] Starting a kind that is already live offers to stop the live one and share from here
- [x] The backend registers the new share first, marked as replacing the old, then closes the old one
- [x] Each viewer in the same room is moved: the old pull closed, the new one created
- [x] If the new share fails to register, the old one carries on untouched
- [x] The first device shows "Now sharing from your other device"
- [x] A hand-over into a different room ends the share in the first room and carries nobody
- [x] Seam 1 tests for each case, including the failed hand-over; a Seam 2 journey

A `replaces` column was tried first, but ticket 23's usage-migration test shares a webcam on an older schema with today's code, and any new `share` column breaks it. The owner was away, so the impeccable skill's questions were not asked; the UI follows `DESIGN.md`, which gained a "Hand-over Offer" section.

- **Protocol.** `shares.start` takes `handOver: true`. Without it, a start while the member's share of that kind is live from another tab or device is refused, as before (ticket 22's auto-restart relies on that). With it, and nothing live, it is an ordinary start. A hand-over from the tab that already sends the share, or while that share is starting, ending or already being handed over, is refused. Two new media changes: `handed-over { kind }` to the tab that lost its share, and `moved { share, offer, video, audio? }` to each viewer carried over, which answers it with `answer` as for `pulled`. `MediaState` (the full state's `media`) gained `sending`: the kinds this tab sends, so a tab back within its grace period can tell its share was handed over while it was away.
- **Order** (`Shares.start`, then `Shares.handOver`). The new share's record and its mark are written, then the media server registers it; the new tab hears `published` and the room `started`. Only then is the old share force-closed, and then each of its pulls: in the same room each viewer is moved (old pull closed quietly, then the new share pulled at the quality they chose if the new share has two, or Auto with a `quality` change if not); into another room every pull is closed and the first room hears `ended`. Within one room the share keeps its name, so the room never hears `ended`, and the sharer's tabs hear one `watchers` for it at the end. The old tab hears `handed-over`.
- **Who is not moved:** the tab the share was handed over to (it may have watched its own share from there; its pull is closed with `unwatched`), a viewer whose socket is not open (in its grace period; its stage comes back without it), and everyone while the usage budget is used up (ticket 24: a move is a new watch). A viewer whose new pull fails, or who leaves meanwhile, gets `unwatched`.
- **Failures.** A failed or abandoned publish closes the new share and drops its mark: the old share is untouched and nobody hears anything. A failed close of the old share keeps it live with its viewers (no longer listed, since the room lists the share by name and the new one is live); once a retry closes it, its viewers get `unwatched` (not moved), its tab `handed-over`, and the room nothing. A restart after the new share went live but before the old one closed is finished after the sweep (`finishHandOvers`): the old share is closed, nobody carried.
- **Listing.** Every query that lists, watches or counts shares (`live`, `liveFrom`, `sharedBy`, `watchers`, `watch`, the one-per-kind check) leaves out a share whose replacement is live (`Shares.current`). `Ended` now carries the sending tab and, for a replaced share, the room its replacement is in; `Instance.sharesEnded` is the one place that tells rooms and tabs, and `Shares.stop` returns an `Ended`.
- **The page.** "Share screen" and "Share webcam" are disabled only while this tab sends that kind ("Screen live", "Webcam live"). While yours is live elsewhere, pressing one opens the offer above the share buttons, or beside the folded rail: "Your screen is already live" / "It's live from another tab or device. Stop it there and share from here?", Cancel and "Share from here" (focused, so Enter goes on). A screen share then goes to the picker, through the first-time notice if due. The offer goes by itself if that share ends meanwhile. The panel shell is now `FootPanel.svelte`, which `ScreenNotice` uses too. The tab that lost a share stops its capture, drops its preview, and shows a dismissible note at the foot: "Now sharing from your other device." (`RoomClient.handedOver`), gone on dismissing or sharing that kind again. A viewer's tab answers `moved` in turn with its other negotiations and swaps the picture, the share staying on the stage.
