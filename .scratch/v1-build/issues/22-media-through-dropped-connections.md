# 22: Media through dropped connections

**What to build:** A dropped signaling connection does not interrupt media. Viewers keep watching through the grace period, a sharer's share stays live and can gain viewers, and a member who returns after the grace period ran out gets their stage or their share back without rebuilding it.

Spec: [Presence and the signaling connection](../spec.md#presence-and-the-signaling-connection), [Shares](../spec.md#shares). User stories 68 to 72.

UI: build with the impeccable skill, following `DESIGN.md`.

**Blocked by:** 06, 15, 20

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [x] Sharer's connection drops while media flows: the share stays live through the grace period, current viewers keep watching and new viewers can start
- [x] If the sharer reconnects in time it is the same share
- [x] The "reconnecting" mark shows on a share's icon when the lost device is the one sending that share
- [x] Viewer's connection drops: the stage keeps playing and shows "Reconnecting…" after about 3 seconds
- [x] A viewer who returns after the grace period ran out has their stage restored to what they were watching, where those shares are still live
- [x] A sharer who returns after the grace period ran out has their share start again by itself, with no picker, if the tab still holds the capture, unless they started it from another device meanwhile
- [x] That sharer's previous viewers are not carried over
- [x] Media falters while the signaling connection is fine: the share stays live for 30 seconds while the browser tries to recover, then ends: the sharer's tab reports the faltering and the recovery, and the backend ends the share
- [x] Seam 1 tests with controlled time; Seam 2 for the marks and the restored stage

- **A sharer's lost connection** already kept the share live and watchable (ticket 06's tab id, ticket 20's cut-off): nothing checks the sharer's socket on a watch, only the share's record and its media connection. `test/dropped.test.ts` now proves it: current viewers hear nothing for 20 seconds, and a new viewer can watch.
- **The mark on a share's icon.** `Signaling` keeps the silent *tabs* of each room (`signaling:silent-tabs`) beside the silent members, worked out in the same `mark` pass, and hands each change to a new `TabSilence` hook. The instance tells the room `shares.reconnecting { share }` / `shares.reconnected { share }` for each live share that tab sends. `ListedShare` gains `reconnecting: true`, so the full state carries it. A share's mark is the sending tab's: Bia's laptop still beating keeps "· reconnecting" off her name but not off her phone's share. When the grace period runs out, the mark goes with the share, unannounced. The room hears the member's mark before the share's. `grace.test.ts`, `restart.test.ts` and `cutoff.test.ts` now expect the share's mark too.
- **The page's two marks** (impeccable skill, following `DESIGN.md`). A share icon whose device is silent breathes like a reconnecting avatar (1.6 s between full and 40%; still at 55% under reduced motion). Its name and tooltip end with ", reconnecting" ("Stop watching Ines's webcam, reconnecting"). There is no colour, as with the name's mark. The rail has no share icons, so it shows only the member's mark, as before. `?sample` shows Duda's webcam marked. The stage's own note: once the room's connection has been unheard for about 3 seconds, a raised pill at the stage's top centre reads "Reconnecting…" beside the small loading mark, in a `role="status"` that is always in the page, over pictures that keep playing. It counts from the moment the page has reason to doubt: a socket that closed, or a heartbeat left unanswered. So it shows 3 seconds after a socket closes, but up to about 8 seconds after a silent drop, since heartbeats go every 5 seconds. Never before the first full state. Screenshots (dark and light) come from `GRAEAE_SCREENSHOTS=<dir>` on the dropped journey.
- **The media connection across a reconnect.** The full state message now carries `media: { connected }` (`MediaState`), outside `RoomView`, since the media area is the tab's own. A tab back within its grace period is the same tab with its connection; one back after it ran out is a new tab without one. The room client reads it in `rejoined`.
  - *Same tab:* the tab drops pictures of shares no longer on its stage, and own shares that ended meanwhile. It re-sends a stop the lost socket never carried. It unwatches and watches again a stage share whose pull never reached it. It tells the instance again whether its media falters.
  - *New tab:* the tab closes the old media connection but keeps its captures (`MediaClient.reset()`). It starts each live own share again on a new connection with the same capture, so no picker opens, unless the full state shows the member's share of that kind live from elsewhere; then the capture stops. It watches again the old stage's shares that are still live, or follows again the member it followed. Each restored watch gets its saved quality back once it is watched (ticket 17's `qualities`; a new watch would be at Auto). A share started again sends the browser family through the same `startRequest`. Previous viewers are not carried over, since the instance ended the old share for them.
- **Ticket 06's leftover**, a `connect` in flight as the socket dropped: the instance notes a connection whose answer found no open socket (`shares:unanswered`). That tab's next `connect` gets a new connection if the old one carries nothing. Otherwise a second `connect` still gets no answer (ticket 10's rule and test stand). On the page, a lost `connect` no longer leaves a rejected promise cached: the next share or watch asks again.
- **Ticket 14's bug** (a click before the room's socket opens stayed on "Starting"): `Connection.send` now queues a message while its socket is opening and sends it on open. It returns false when the connection is lost and no socket is opening, and the room client then fails that request at once, so the share shows "didn't start" instead of hanging. Nothing is kept for a later socket, as before. Seam 2 holds a reload's socket open-pending through Playwright, clicks "Share webcam", then lets it open (red before the fix).
- **Media that falters.** New requests `media.faltering` and `media.recovered`. The instance keeps the first `faltering` moment per tab (`shares:faltering`); 30 seconds later the alarm (one more moment in `Instance.schedule`) ends the tab's live shares through the ordinary stop's forced close. **Decision:** what the tab watches is left alone, since the spec says the *share* ends. A connection left empty is forgotten, and the tab hears `media.dropped`, so its next share makes a new connection. `recovered`, a clean leave or a grace period running out clears the wait. **The browser:** `connectionstatechange` to "disconnected" or "failed" reports faltering, and "connected" reports recovery. No ICE restart is tried: it would need a renegotiation through the instance that Cloudflare has not been shown to take. The fake has `graeaeFakeMedia.falter()` / `recover()` and counts `connections`.
- **Tests.** `test/dropped.test.ts` (Seam 1, 14): the share through the grace period with a new viewer; the share's mark with another device heard, going on reconnect with the same share and its watchers, and going with the share; `media.connected` fresh, in time and after; the lost `connect` answer; a new connection, a fresh start with no watchers, and a refused second start when another device started it meanwhile; faltering ending the share at 30 s with `dropped` then a new connection; recovering in time; counting again after recovering; a faltering viewer keeping its watching. `test/browser/dropped.spec.ts` (Seam 2; members Kim, Kit and Ines, since ticket 17 took Lou): the early click; a member dropping for 35 seconds while watching a webcam and a screen at 720p and sharing both, with "Reconnecting…" on her stage, the marks on the owner's icons, her leaving, then her stage restored (720p included), both shares live again with no picker on a second media connection, and nobody watching them; the faltering and recovery frames on the socket. Each journey closes its pages before its browsers.

**Needs the owner:** after ticket 13's deploy, on Windows 11 with current Chrome or Helium, two browsers on different networks.
1. *Media that falters* (box 8): while sharing, block the sharer's UDP and TCP to Cloudflare for a few seconds (a firewall rule, or unplug and replug the cable quickly), then for over 30 seconds. The short drop should recover with the share still live. The long one should end the share for the viewers, and the next share should start on a new connection. If Chrome reaches "failed" and never recovers from a short drop, an ICE restart (`restartIce()` and a renegotiation through the instance) is the next thing to build; graeae tries none today.
2. *Media through a lost socket:* while a viewer watches, cut only the signaling socket (DevTools can't do this alone; closing the Wi-Fi for 15 seconds does both, so watch that the picture freezes at most briefly and resumes). The viewer's stage should say "Reconnecting…" and keep the picture.
3. *Coming back after 30 seconds:* drop a sharer for 40 seconds. Their share should start again on its own with no picker, using the same capture on a new `RTCPeerConnection`. That has only run on the fake. If Chrome refuses to send a track that an old connection sent, `BrowserMediaClient.reset()` is where to look.

**Checked on real media by an agent** on 2026-11-17, on `graeae-test` (the deploy of `9f71b02`), at the owner's request. It used two members invited for it, "bot" sharing and "bot viewer" watching. Each browser was a headless Chromium 157 on Linux, with Chromium's synthetic camera, in a network namespace of its own (slirp4netns), whose firewall (nft) the agent drove. Both browsers' media went over UDP to Cloudflare at `141.101.90.0`, and signaling went to the Worker's own addresses, so each could be cut alone. The harness is not in the repo.

- *Media that falters* (box 8) holds.
  - A 5-second cut of the sharer's media never left "connected": the picture came back about 2 s after the network.
  - A 10-second cut went "disconnected" at +7 s, and the tab sent `media.faltering`. On the network's return it sent `media.recovered`, and the viewer had a picture again within 3 s, still watching.
  - A 45-second cut went "disconnected" at +6 s and "failed" at +16 s. The share ended for the viewer 36 s after the faltering, not 30 s: about 6 s late, from the alarm or the forced close. The tab heard `media.dropped`, and the next share made a new connection that the viewer watched at 20 fps.
  - **Open:** a 20-second cut never recovered. Chrome gives a connection up as "failed" at about 17 s and does not try again by itself, so the share ended at the 30 s mark. Any drop between about 17 and 30 seconds ends the share. Ticket 25's `media.restart` (an ICE restart through the instance) is the likely way to recover; the owner decides whether to build it.
- *Coming back after 30 seconds* (40 s with the sharer's whole network cut) holds.
  - The viewer saw the "reconnecting" marks, then the member left and the share ended.
  - On the sharer's return the webcam started again with no new capture: `getUserMedia` was called once in the whole run. It went out on a second `RTCPeerConnection` at 20 fps, with nobody's watching carried over. Chrome sends a track that an old connection sent.
- *Media through a lost socket* found **two bugs**, both fixed here.
  1. **A real network drop gave the tab a new identity.** When a network drops, the Worker keeps its end of the socket open but silent until the grace period runs out. The instance took a tab back only from tabs it had seen close, so a page reconnecting in time got a new tab, with `media.connected: false`.
     - A viewer cut from signaling for 15 s lost its picture for about 3 s on reconnect, while it watched again on a new connection. The picture had played throughout the cut, and "Reconnecting…" showed after 3 s, as it should.
     - A sharer cut from signaling for 15 s, with media fine, saw the share of that kind as "live from elsewhere", so the page stopped the capture. The share stayed listed, marked "reconnecting" with nothing behind it, until the old socket's grace period ran out, about 25 s later.
  2. **A page opened afresh took the device's lost tab.** This happens on a reload, or the tab opened again, within the grace period after an unclean close. The page got `connected: true` for a media connection it never held, its `connect` went unanswered ("already"), and it could neither watch nor share.

  **The fix.** The full state now names the tab (`{ type: "state", tab }`), and the page names it again when it reconnects (`roomSocketPath(address, tab)`, a `?tab=` query).
  - The instance takes that tab back whether it is lost or its old socket is still open and silent. The old socket is closed with the new `TAKEN_BACK_CLOSE_CODE` (4409) and does not count as a leave.
  - A tab named from another device is not taken.
  - A page that names no tab is a new one: the device-and-room guess is gone. The member stays present all the same, since the lost tab keeps them present until its grace period runs out, and that tab's shares and watching end then.
  - The spec's presence section says so.
  - Tests: Seam 1 in `test/dropped.test.ts` covers a silent old socket, another device naming the tab, and a page opened afresh. Seam 2 covers a sharer's silent 12-second drop keeping her share, her one media connection and her viewer (red before the fix). Sixteen Seam 1 tests that reconnect a tab now name it, through the test `Tab`'s new `id`.
  - `pnpm test` (Seam 1, 427 tests; Seam 2, 36 journeys), `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm lint` pass.
