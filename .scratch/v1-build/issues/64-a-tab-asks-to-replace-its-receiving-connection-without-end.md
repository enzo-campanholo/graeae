## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** `unavailable` for a receiving connection says why. The tab stops asking when there is nothing to replace, and backs off when the media server failed.

**Current behavior:**
When the browser gives up the receiving connection, the room client's `recover("receiving")` asks the instance to `replace` it. It asks again 2 seconds after each try that returns false, for as long as the connection is still failed and still the tab's. The instance answers `unavailable` for three different causes:
- the tab's member is not allowed in the room;
- the instance has no receiving connection of the tab's to replace;
- the media server or the relay failed.

The first two will never succeed. The third may. Every failed try costs a `sessions/new` call to the media server, a relay credential mint, and warn lines from the instance and the tab. Those lines count against the free plan's daily Workers Logs allowance, past which lines are dropped.

**Desired behavior:**
- **`unavailable` gives a reason.** For the receiving connection, it tells apart at least "the media server or the relay failed" from "nothing to replace" and "not allowed". The instance's log line names the reason on its own, rather than the one combined `why` it has now.
- **When asking cannot help, the tab stops.** On "nothing to replace" or "not allowed", the tab lets go of its receiving connection as it does on `dropped`: it sends its final usage total and resets that side. It asks no more. Its next watch makes a new receiving connection.
- **When the media server or relay failed, the tab backs off.** The tab keeps asking while the connection is still failed. It waits 2 seconds after the first failed try, doubles the wait after each one after that, and never waits more than 30 seconds. A replacement that succeeds, or a connection that recovers by itself, resets the wait.
- Decided in triage: there is no hard end on the receiving side. Backing off to 30 seconds keeps a tab left open on a down media server to about two tries a minute, and a connection that comes back later is replaced without a reload.
- The sending connection's restart, and its 2-second retry and 30-second rule, are unchanged. If `connect` gets the same reason field, the tab handles a refused `connect` as it does now.

**Key interfaces:**
- The socket's media change `{ type: "unavailable"; connection }`, which gains a reason. v1 is unreleased, so the protocol changes without keeping the old form (ADR 0005, "No compatibility").
- The instance's handler for `media.replace`, and `Shares.replace`, whose null result now stands for two different causes.
- The room client's `replace()` and `recover()`, and the retry wait they share with the sending side (`RESTART_AGAIN_MS`).

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Seam 1: `replace` is answered with the right reason in each of three cases:
  - the fake media server fails `sessions/new`;
  - the tab has no receiving connection on record;
  - the member was removed from the room.
- [ ] Browser journey or room-client test on the fakes, with replacements failing on the media server: while the connection stays failed, the tab's tries are spaced 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 and 30 seconds. Once a try succeeds, a later failure starts again from 2 seconds.
- [ ] On "nothing to replace", the tab sends no further `replace`. Its stage is emptied as on `dropped`, and its next watch asks for a new receiving connection.
- [ ] The sending connection's retries are unchanged, and their existing tests still pass.
- [ ] Spec.md's paragraph on a receiving connection the browser gives up, and the headers of the files changed, describe the reasons and the backing off.
- [ ] `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint` and `pnpm test` are green.

**Out of scope:**
- The sending connection's restart and its 30-second rule.
- 62's hand-over and eviction cases, and 63's usage overlap.
- Why the media server or the relay fails.

### Done

> *This was generated by AI during implementation.*

**What changed.**
- `unavailable` carries `reason`: `"failed"` (the media server or the relay failed), `"nothing to replace"`, or `"not allowed"` (`Unavailable` in `src/shared/socket/media.ts`). `connect` answers with `"failed"`, its only refusal; its silence when not allowed is unchanged.
- `Shares.replace` returns `"nothing to replace"` when the tab has no receiving connection on record, without asking the media server anything; null now means only that the media server or the relay failed. The instance's `media.unavailable` line gives one `why` per reason, and "not allowed" now answers the tab instead of leaving its request unanswered.
- The room client:
  - **Failed:** it keeps its connection and asks again 2, 4, 8, 16, 30, 30… seconds apart (`REPLACE_AGAIN_MS`, `REPLACE_AGAIN_MAX_MS`).
  - **Nothing to replace, or not allowed:** it lets go of the receiving connection (its pictures dropped, then `resetMedia`; the final total went out before the ask), and asks no more.
  - **Ending a wait early:** a connection that recovers by itself ends `recover`'s wait at once (`Side.wake`), so the next time it is given up starts again from 2 seconds. `recover` no longer waits after a try that let go of the side.
  - **Sending side:** restarts keep their 2 seconds.
- spec.md's paragraph, and the headers of `room-client.svelte.ts`, `media.ts`, `shares.ts`, `usage.ts` and `log.ts`, state the reasons and the backing off.

**Deviation: "the member was removed from the room".** It cannot be answered "not allowed" over the socket. Each way of losing access cuts the tab in the same synchronous step as the storage change, and `Signaling` neither reads from nor sends to a cut tab (`tabOf` checks `cutOff`, which is kept in the socket's attachment). Instead, Seam 1 checks that a removed member's `replace`, sent while the cut-off's closes are held, asks the media server nothing, mints nothing, writes no `media.unavailable` line, and that the tab hears only `gone`. The "not allowed" branch stays as defensive code, and no test reaches it.
