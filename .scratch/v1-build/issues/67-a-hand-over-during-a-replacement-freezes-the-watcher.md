# 67: A hand-over during a replacement freezes the watcher

**Status:** resolved

**Blocked by:** none

**What was seen:** on `graeae-test` (ticket 62's check, 2026-11-24), a watcher's receiving connection was replaced while the share it watched was handed over to the sharer's other tab. The watcher's picture froze for good. Only a reload brought it back; unwatching and watching again did nothing (ticket 62's comment has the timeline). `scripts/real-media/repro-67.ts` reproduced it on version `2a729393` (build `063611c-dirty`, ticket 66's fix). With `fail receiving` 0.1 to 0.5 s after "Share from here", 5 of 7 tries were red. In two of them the picture froze on the stage. In the other three the share left the stage altogether.

**Why:** three failures. All of them happen while the hand-over closes the old share's tracks, when `gone(old share)` is true but the watcher's old watch has neither left nor begun to close.

1. **The freeze.** The replacement's pull of the old share returns in that window, or after it. `Shares.replaceNext` closed that pull and called `forgetIfEmpty(replacement)` while the tab was still open. That deleted the empty replacement, the step came out `"stale"`, and `Instance.replaceStep` sends nothing for `"stale"`. The move then pulled the new share onto the old connection, which the tab was leaving. The tab waited for a `pulled` or `replaced` that never came, and every later watch queued behind it.
2. **The share leaves the stage (older than 66).** A step that finds the old watch's share gone in that window called `leave()`. That told the tab `unwatched`, so the hand-over had no watcher left to move (`handover.done` with `watchers: 0`). A pull the media server failed because the hand-over had just closed the track went the same way.
3. **The media server's 406, seen after fixing 1 and 2 (version `d6976c93`, 4 of 9 red).** The replacement's pull of the old share returned an offer, and the instance force-closed that pull without sending the offer to the tab. The media server takes no other pull into a session until the browser answers the last offer, and a close does not change that. The move's pull of the new share into the same session, 5 s later, got `406 The current signaling state is expecting a remote answer: renegotiation is expected`, and the share left the stage. Now in `docs/media-server.md`.

**Combined code review of 66 and 67 against `063611c` (2026-11-24), and what it changed.**

The review ran on two axes, standards and spec. It found two places where 67's passing-over undid what 66 and 67 meant to fix. Both came from a replacement that could end while a hand-over of a share on its stage was still under way.

- **66's empty replacement came back.** A replacement passed over the watch the hand-over was closing and ended carrying nothing. `letGo` kept it while that watch was still open, and the tab heard `replaced`. If the hand-over then closed the watch rather than moving it (into another room, the tab not connected, the move's pull failed), the tab kept a never-joined, empty receiving connection. Its next watch would get the 410.
- **67's failure 2 remained with two or more watchers.** `handOver` takes the old share off `closingShares` once its tracks are closed, then moves its watchers one at a time. Say watcher B's replacement ended while A's move was still pulling. `finishReplacement` then closed B's old watch without `moving`: B heard `unwatched`, and B's move found nothing left to move.

**Fix (`src/instance/shares.ts`).**
- A hand-over is recorded in `handingOver` from the close of its tracks until each watcher is moved or closed, together with the tabs that watched the old share when it began.
- `replaceNext` doesn't finish while a hand-over involving its tab is under way. It waits for it, forgets what it passed over, and looks again. So the moved watch is pulled as one more step, as `spec.md` always said. A closed watch leaves nothing behind, and an empty replacement ends `dropped`. If the hand-over's close fails, the old share is pulled again rather than taken off the stage.
- `finishReplacement` leaves an old-connection watch alone while its share has a hand-over under way, not only while its tracks are closing. That covers a hand-over that begins just as a replacement ends, which is now the only way to get a `moved` after `replaced`.
- Smaller changes:
  - `gone(share)` and `shareGone(id)` are now one `gone(id)`;
  - `passed` is renamed `passedOver`;
  - the protocol comment on `pulled` (`src/shared/socket/media.ts`) and `spec.md` now say the replacement waits for the hand-over.

**Seam 1 (`test/replacing.test.ts`).**
- New: "waits for the hand-over, then lets go of a replacement that ends carrying nothing once the move of its one share fails" (c1).
- New: "keeps the share on the stage of a watcher whose replacement is made while the hand-over moves another watcher" (c2, with Caio and the owner watching).
- Reworked for the new order, a `pulled` step where there used to be a `moved` after `replaced`:
  - the move's pull to the old connection returning after the replacement began;
  - the two `it.each` cases;
