Test plan
  1. Baseline. Sharer on Chrome with a demanding game running in borderless mode, H.264, 12 Mbps. Viewer on Chrome. Let it run 5 minutes of real play. Save a note with the game's fps before and while sharing.
  2. Firefox viewer. Same share, a viewer opens it in Firefox.
  3. Firefox sharer. Share from Firefox with the game running, once with VP9 and once with H.264. A Chromium viewer watches.
  4. Three screens, then four. Everyone shares; one person watches all the others. If only two people are around, one of them shares several windows or monitors.
  5. High bitrate. Share at 15 and then 20 Mbps and see what the viewer actually receives.
  6. Forced relay. Tick "Force relay" on both sides and repeat the baseline for 2 minutes.
  7. Application audio. Share the game's window with audio on: a normal game, one in exclusive fullscreen, one started through a launcher. The viewer says whether they hear only the game.
  8. Helium, Vivaldi, Brave. Share from each with the game running, H.264, default browser settings. Check "encoder" says hardware. Repeat the application-audio check in each.
  9. Exclusive fullscreen. Share the whole screen, then put the game in exclusive fullscreen. Does the viewer's picture freeze?
  10. Cut-off. While watching, press "Cut at source". Do it 5 times (the sharer shares again each time). Then try "Cut my watching" a few times.
  11. Relay revoked. Watch with "Force relay" ticked, then press "Revoke my relay credential" and leave the tile open.
Type or pick the matching "Test run" before each step so the log can tell them apart.
